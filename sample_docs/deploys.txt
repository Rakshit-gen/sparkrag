Deploy process

Deploys go out through the release pipeline, not manually. Merging to main
triggers a staging deploy automatically. Promoting to production requires
a second approval from someone outside your team, tagged in the release
channel.

Rollbacks are one click from the deploy dashboard and take about ninety
seconds to take effect. If a rollback does not clear the error rate within
five minutes, page the on-call lead instead of retrying the rollback again.

Freeze windows apply during the last week of each quarter. Only sev-1 fixes
are allowed to deploy during a freeze, and they need a director sign-off.
